Chord Sheet Builder

Fill in beats and chords — alignment is handled for you.

Two ways to save:
  • Save draft — temporary backup in this browser while you transcribe. Won’t appear on the site.
  • Save .md — the real song file for _songs/. Commit and push to publish on the site.
  • Section repeat — use the Repeat field (e.g. 2x) to play that section twice. Saved as III 2x on the section line.
  • Slash chords — use C/G for chord-over-chord (formats as C/G, Am/G, etc.).

Sections: I Intro · II Verse · III Chorus · IV Bridge · V Outro
